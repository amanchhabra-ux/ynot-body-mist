Ordering and payment

How do I place an order?

Pick your scents on the home page, add them to your order, fill in your name, mobile number, email and delivery address, and pay on the site. Prefer to talk first? Message us on WhatsApp and we will take the order there.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets. Payments are handled by Razorpay, so your card details, UPI PIN and OTPs go straight to them and never reach us.

How much does it cost?

Every scent is ₹599 for a 100 ml bottle, inclusive of all taxes. Delivery is free anywhere in India, so ₹599 is the whole price.

Money left my account but my order did not confirm. What now?

Nothing has been taken by us — the payment was never captured, and your bank reverses it automatically, usually within 5 to 7 working days. Send us the payment reference and we will follow it up with Razorpay alongside you.

Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Orders are packed and handed to the courier within 1 to 3 working days, and delivery then usually takes 4 to 8 working days depending on where you are. Metro addresses are at the quicker end. Full details are in our Shipping Policy.

How can I track my order?

You get a confirmation email the moment you pay, and a WhatsApp message with tracking details as soon as your parcel leaves us.

Do you ship outside India?

Not yet. We ship across India only. Fragrance is restricted on air cargo, so a few remote pincodes may take longer — if yours cannot be reached, we will contact you and refund in full.

Returns and cancellations

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, if you message us before it ships — we will cancel it and refund the full amount. Once it has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but the return terms still apply.

What if my bottle arrives damaged, leaking or wrong?

Tell us within 7 days of delivery and send a photo. We will replace it or refund it in full, whichever you prefer. See our Refund Policy.

Can I return a scent I did not like?

For hygiene reasons an opened bottle cannot be resold, so we cannot take back a used bottle because the scent was not to your taste. Read the scent notes before ordering, or message us for a recommendation — we are happy to help you pick.

How long does a refund take?

We start approved refunds within 2 working days, back to the original payment method. Your bank then usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show the money.

The mist spray

What is a mist spray, and how is it different from perfume?

A mist spray is a lighter fragrance than perfume — less concentrated, so it sits closer to the skin and you can reapply it through the day without it becoming overpowering.

Is a mist spray the same as a body spray?

Close, but not quite. “Body spray” usually means a gas-propelled aerosol, often doubling as a deodorant. Ynot is a fragrance mist: a fine, light spray made for scent rather than for sweat, that you can use freely through the day.

What scents are there?

Four. Black Bloom is floral, elegant and unforgettable. Amour Belle is romantic, dreamy and charming. Legacy is fresh, bold and fearless. Dark Rogue is woody, mysterious and addictive. Black Bloom and Amour Belle are made for girls, Legacy and Dark Rogue for boys — but wear whichever one feels like you.

How should I use it?

Shake it, then spray from about a hand’s length onto skin or clothes. Reapply whenever you like. It is for external use only: keep it away from your eyes and from flames, and store it somewhere cool and dry.
